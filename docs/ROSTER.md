# Enemy roster proposal (round 8)

Nothing here goes into the game until the designer has been through it (deck roster2).

## 1. The Wildwood
A forest. The new first dungeon. Floor 1, the forest edge: wolves, slimes, bandit scouts, brambles. Floor 2, the bandit camp: bandits, snares, campfires. Floor 3, the old grove: ents, sproutlings, wisps, grasping roots.

- **Wolf** (new): Fast pack hunter. Circles a hero, then lunges. Its howl turns the whole pack onto one hero.
- **Dire wolf** (variant of Wolf): Black and bigger. Pounces from far off and pins a hero for a second.
- **Slime** (in): As now: splits in two when it dies.
- **Moss slime** (variant of Slime): Dark green. Leaves a trail of moss that slows; splits into three.
- **Goblin** (in): Small knife fighter, comes in groups.
- **Orange goblin** (variant of Goblin): Yours: 1.5x as fast, weaves in and out of the fight and never stands still.
- **Bandit** (new): Sword and buckler. Blocks the first hit, and each hit steals a few coins: kill it to get them back.
- **Bandit archer** (new): Shoots from behind cover and moves to a new spot after every shot.
- **Poacher** (variant of Bandit archer): Green hood. Lays snares on the floor and fires a net that pins a hero.
- **Bandit brute** (new): Big club. A slow wind-up slam that sends heroes flying.
- **Thornback boar** (new): Charges in a straight line. If it hits a wall or a rock it staggers itself.
- **Sproutling** (new): A small walking sapling, always in groups. Roots itself to heal if left alone.
- **Ent** (new): A huge slow tree. Slams roots up in a line. Takes extra from fire, and drops sproutlings when hit hard.
- **Wisp** (new): A floating light. Blinks about, zaps, and heals beasts near it: kill it first.
- **The Alpha** (mini-boss): A giant wolf. Calls packs in waves; its howl makes every wolf faster for a while.
- **The Bandit Chief** (mini-boss): Smoke bombs that hide the room, a fan of thrown knives, and a whistle that brings archers.
- **The Elder Ent** (boss): Root cages that pin, falling branches you see coming, a grove of sproutlings that feed it. Set it alight and it rampages: faster, but burning down.

## 2. The Warren
Goblin tunnels. Floor 1, the tunnels: goblins, rats, snares. Floor 2, the war camp: hobgoblins, orcs, wolf riders, firepits. Floor 3, the deep halls: trolls, shamans, the war drums.

- **Goblin, Hobgoblin, Orc, Orc shaman, Troll, Rat** (in): As now (the hobgoblin's war cry, the orc's charge, the shaman's fireballs, the troll's regrowth).
- **Goblin bomber** (variant of Goblin): Red cap. Lobs bombs with a lit fuse; they sit a moment before they blow.
- **Wolf rider** (new): A goblin on a wolf. Charges through the party; when the wolf dies the goblin fights on foot.
- **Frost shaman** (variant of Orc shaman): Blue. Chills instead of burns, and plants ice totems that slow everyone near.
- **Rock troll** (variant of Troll): Grey. Throws boulders. Stone skin shrugs off damage until it's staggered.
- **Drummer** (new): A goblin with a war drum. Does no damage; everything near it attacks faster while it plays.
- **The Beastmaster** (mini-boss): A hobgoblin with a whip and three wolves. The wolves get stronger each time one dies.
- **The Twin Trolls** (mini-boss): Two trolls that heal each other. They have to go down close together.
- **The Warboss** (boss): A proper fight this time: charges, a spinning cleaver, and war drums round the room that call waves until they're smashed.

## 3. The Sewers
Under the city. Floor 1, the drains: rats, slimes, gas. Floor 2, the canals: lizardmen, crabs, deep water. Floor 3, the cistern: the drowned, crocs, the dark.

- **Rat, Slime, Lizardman, Lizard blade** (in): As now (the lizardman's tail spin soaks).
- **Plague rat** (variant of Rat): Sickly green. Bursts into a poison cloud when it dies.
- **Sludge slime** (variant of Slime): Brown. Leaves oil wherever it goes, and burns fiercely if lit.
- **Croc** (new): Bites and holds, then rolls, dragging the hero. Fast in water, slow out of it.
- **Sewer crab** (new): Armoured from the front. Sidesteps, then grabs with a pincer: hit it from behind.
- **The Drowned** (new): A waterlogged corpse. Grabs a hero and drags them toward the water. Soaks.
- **Leech** (new): Tiny. Latches onto a hero and drains them until an ally knocks it off.
- **The Rat King** (mini-boss): A knot of rats. Splits into a swarm when hit and re-forms; hurt the swarm to hurt the king.
- **The Lizard Chief** (mini-boss): Spear and tide: floods part of the room, and everything in the water with him is faster.
- **The Thing in the Cistern** (boss): Never fully seen. Tentacles rise from the canals to slam and grab; the water level rises through the fight.

## 4. The Crypt
Old graves under the hill. Floor 1, the catacombs: rattlers, bone hounds, grave mist. Floor 2, the chapel: acolytes, cursed armour, ghosts. Floor 3, the royal tombs: golden dead, wraiths, the King.

- **Rattler (sword, bow, spear, mace), Gaper, Brute** (in): As now.
- **Ghost** (in): Now gives Curse (no healing, more damage taken) instead of chill.
- **Banshee** (in): Moves here from the Frost Hollow as well: her scream slows and curses.
- **Golden skeleton** (variant of Rattler): Yours: drops double gold, and summons golden bones all round it that home in, go through heroes and hit 1.5x as hard.
- **Bone hound** (new): A fast skeletal dog. Falls apart when killed, then pulls itself together once.
- **Acolyte** (new): A hooded cultist. Raises fallen skeletons back up: kill it first.
- **Cursed armour** (new): An empty suit of armour. Collapses when beaten, and stands back up unless its helmet is hit while down.
- **Wraith** (variant of Ghost): Dark. Its curse also steals the healing meant for its target.
- **The Grave Warden** (mini-boss): As now, tuned up: raises a ring of dead and slams the ground.
- **The Ossuary** (mini-boss): A heap of bones that builds itself bigger from every skeleton that dies near it.
- **The Bone King** (boss): Stronger: a fourth phase after the dark one where he raises his whole court at once, and his lance leaves a wall of bone behind it.

## 5. The Frost Hollow
Ice caves. Floor 1, the snowfield: frost wolves, snowmen, bats. Floor 2, the web caves: spiders, ice slimes, frozen pools. Floor 3, the glacier heart: yetis, ogres, blizzards.

- **Bat, Snowman, Hunter spider, Momma spider, Ogre** (in): As now.
- **Frost wolf** (variant of Wolf): White. Its bite chills, and a pack can freeze a hero solid.
- **Ice slime** (variant of Slime): Pale blue. Leaves ice that makes heroes slip, and shatters into shards when killed.
- **Frost spider** (variant of Hunter spider): Its webs chill instead of only slowing.
- **Yeti** (new): Throws snow boulders from range, and roars to stagger anything close.
- **Icicle sprite** (new): Hangs back and drops icicles from above: a shadow shows where.
- **The Brood Queen** (mini-boss): A giant spider. Webs up the exits, and her eggs hatch unless they're broken.
- **Old Frost** (mini-boss): An elder yeti. Rolls giant snowballs across the room that grow as they go.
- **The Winter Queen** (boss): An ice mage. Walls of ice split the party, a blizzard hides the room, and she must be reached while her mirror images shatter.

## 6. The Ember Deep
Fire and brimstone. Floor 1, the ash wastes: imps, cinder bats, burning ground. Floor 2, the forges: devils, magma golems, lava flows. Floor 3, the pit: demons, dragonborn, the drake.

- **Imp, Fire slime, Devil, Demon, Dragonborn** (in): As now.
- **Fire spider** (variant of Hunter spider): Yours: burns instead of poisons, and spits fireballs.
- **Hellhound** (variant of Wolf): Leaves a trail of fire where it runs, and breathes a short cone of flame.
- **Cinder bat** (variant of Bat): Dives at a hero and explodes.
- **Magma golem** (new): Slow, and leaves fire where it walks. Soak or chill it and it cracks: stunned and brittle.
- **Ash wraith** (variant of Ghost): Throws ash that blinds: a blinded hero can only hit what's right next to it.
- **The Pit Lord** (mini-boss): As now, tuned up: a whip of fire and rifts that spit imps.
- **The Dragonborn Champion** (mini-boss): A duellist. Marks one hero and hunts them; breaks off only when staggered.
- **Cinder, the Old Drake** (boss): A dragon. Breath in a sweeping cone, a wing gust that scatters the party, and passes overhead that strafe a line of fire.

## 7. The Gilded Spire
A temple in the clouds. Floor 1, the steps: cherubs, sun blades, sigils. Floor 2, the choir halls: angels, choir sprites, light beams. Floor 3, the sanctum: seraphs, golems, the fallen.

- **Cherub, Angel, Seraph** (in): Now give Radiance (slower, much weaker attacks; at full, a glowing aura that spreads it and stops you attacking).
- **Fallen angel** (variant of Angel): Dark wings. Gives Curse instead of Radiance.
- **Sun blade** (new): A floating sword. Spins through the party in straight lines; can't be staggered.
- **Choir sprite** (new): Sings: everything in its song heals. Silenced by a stagger.
- **Gilded golem** (new): A gold statue. Shots from the front bounce back at the shooter.
- **The Twin Heralds** (mini-boss): One of light, one fallen. Each heals in the other's aura, so they have to be pulled apart.
- **The Archon** (mini-boss): As now, tuned up: pillars of light that sweep the room like clock hands.
- **The Hollow Saint** (boss): A statue with nothing inside. Radiance floods the room in pulses, and the only shade is behind the pillars it hasn't yet broken.

## 8. The Sunken Dunes (new)
A desert tomb. Floor 1, the dunes: scarabs, cobras, quicksand. Floor 2, the buried halls: jackal guards, mummies, dart traps. Floor 3, the sun chamber: sand mages, wurms, the Pharaoh.

- **Scarab** (new): Small, in swarms. Burrows and pops up under a hero.
- **Cobra** (new): Spits poison from range and rears back out of reach.
- **Golden cobra** (variant of Cobra): Its spit blinds instead; drops extra gold.
- **Jackal guard** (new): Spear and shield. Sidesteps a blow, then counters.
- **Mummy** (new): Slow. Its wrappings pull a hero in, and its touch curses. Burns easily.
- **Scorpion** (new): Pincers hold a hero still for the sting, which staggers.
- **Sand mage** (new): Raises a sandstorm that shortens everyone's reach inside it.
- **Sand wurm** (new): Burrows, then erupts under a hero: the ground shakes first.
- **The Tomb Guard** (mini-boss): A giant jackal statue that wakes. Slow, heavy, and the floor traps are on its side.
- **The Wurm Mother** (mini-boss): Dives in and out of the sand; only her tail can be hurt while she's under.
- **The Sun Pharaoh** (boss): Burning sun discs that cross the room, a curse on whoever stands in his gaze, and a guard of mummies he calls from the walls.

## 9. The Clockwork Foundry (new)
A factory that never stopped. Floor 1, the scrapyard: gear sprites, bomb bots, crushers. Floor 2, the assembly line: automatons, turrets, conveyors. Floor 3, the engine room: tesla coils, smiths, steam.

- **Gear sprite** (new): A rolling cog. Bounces off walls and through the party.
- **Bomb bot** (new): Walks up, ticks, and explodes. Knock it away in time and it blows up its own side.
- **Automaton** (new): A sword construct. Hits hard, then overheats and stands open for a moment.
- **Steam turret** (new): Doesn't move. Turns slowly to fire: get behind it.
- **Tesla coil** (new): Shock that chains between heroes; much worse on anyone wet.
- **Repair drone** (new): Floats about mending constructs. Kill it first.
- **Foundry smith** (new): A big brute with a red-hot hammer. Every hit burns.
- **The Foreman** (mini-boss): Rides a walking crane. Drops crates on heroes and winds up broken constructs again.
- **The Twin Pistons** (mini-boss): Two great hammers that take turns slamming halves of the room.
- **The Great Engine** (boss): Doesn't move. Arms that sweep, vents that scald, and a core that only opens when it overheats.
