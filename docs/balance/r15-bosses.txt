!! FIRST: A BUG THAT DECIDES FIVE OF THE NINE (found here, not fixed: the lane was told to change nothing) !!
Round 15's Mob (lane N) made sim.js send a death, a blow's share and a status from a body to its lead through
`u.partOf` (sim.js kill: "if (u.partOf) u = u.partOf; // the four fall as one"; hurt's pool; addStatus; step's
`body`). The designed bosses' parts carry `partOf` too (boss-kit.js part(): `p.partOf = core`), so breaking a boss's
part now KILLS THE BOSS. Seen: the Great Engine at 5409 of 5873 health dies the frame one of its hands breaks
(`--watch`), every fight over in about 22 s with the log reading "phase 1 at 0% (3s)". It touches every boss with
breakable parts: the Great Engine (hands, valve), Cinder, the Great Ooze, the Gilded Seraph, the Sand Wurm.
A one-line-per-site fix, measured below as "fixed" (applied to a copy of the code only, not committed): send to the
lead only when the lead is a Mob's, `u.partOf && u.partOf.bodies` (kill, hurt's pool, addStatus, step's body).
The campaign was run again with that copy (seeds 1-4) for the levels the fixed game meets each boss at.
