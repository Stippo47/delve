# Art round 2: the four artists' notes (the designer's words, per design)

Each artist reads `design/briefs/round2-brief.md` first, then only their own section.

## A. Knights -> design/class-round2/knight/
- **Paladin** (knight design-1): "Hand when holding shield should be hidden. Longer/bigger sword, angled inwards."
- **Old Guard** (design-2): "Sword short. Holding pose is weird. Cape wider and straight on. Beard not meshing with balding hairline."
- **Dame** (design-4): "Good. Fix cape to be straight. Hand on shield hidden. Golden crown or accent on head. Bigger cooler sword. The hair shadow onto face isn't orange like the hair. I'm not talking about the gradient." (The shadow the hair casts on the face should be a warm hair-coloured shade, not grey/brown.)
- **Bastion** (design-3): "Bad. I can't tell what's going on. Redo. 3 versions." Three new tank-knight designs (bastion-a/b/c), each readable at 40px: you must be able to tell helm, shoulders, shield and weapon apart.

## B. Skeletons -> design/class-round2/skeleton/
Rules for all of them: **give eye sockets** (this overrides "no eyes" for skeletons), **no light/dark split down the middle** of the skull, **remove the rib cage**, and the **nose hole the right way up** (an upside-down heart / triangle pointing up, as on a real skull). No hood-like shadow at the top of the skull.
- **Rattler** (design-1): "Remove rib cage. Give eye sockets. Then make variants. Including one without a bottom jaw. Try without the split down the middle." -> rattler plus 2-3 variants (one jawless).
- **Gaper** (design-2): "Not horrible but why is there a hood shadow. Try no split. Eye sockets. Isn't the nose triangle the wrong way around?"
- **Brute** (design-3): "Similar edits as last 2. Remove the arm bones."
- **Hollow** (design-4): "You get the idea." (same edits)
