# Barbarians, round 4 (fixes to the picks)

Built from `design/class-round3/barbarian/` by `_src/F/barbs.py`. Parts and pivots unchanged. Renders in `renders/`.
Hand rule everywhere: each weapon fist wraps the handle (long along it, knuckles down the outer side, turned to it).

## Wildmane (scar): `barbarian-wildmane-scar.svg`
- "That's a she": made only the scar version, as asked. The forked beard is gone (it read as a man). In its place
  two long locks of the same rust hair fall from beside her face down onto the mantle. Fringe, mane, wolf-fur mantle
  and greatsword kept. No facial features.
- Scar, "just one solid darker shade, not white": one solid stroke of darker skin (#C47F62), no white core.
- Move (the designer's, from the roster): **Rage** - for a short time she takes less damage and attacks and moves faster.
- Weak: with no face, "woman" rests on the long locks and the missing beard. The mantle is the same broad one as
  before; a slimmer mantle would help but would change her look, so I left it.

## Bearskin: `barbarian-bearskin.svg`
- "Worse club?": a new club. Straight and bigger, pale wood thickening to a rounded end, five iron spikes (two each
  side, one at the tip), three nail heads and a dark wrapped handle. It sits low at the front-right, under the paw,
  and comes out forward. The round-2 war axe stays the fallback if this still doesn't read.
- Suggested move: **Maul** - a heavy overhead smash that stuns the target.
- Weak: from some facings the club overlaps the paw's claws.

## Warhelm: `barbarian-warhelm.svg`
- "Hammer maybe a bit small, 15% bigger": the hammer head is exactly 15% bigger, on a slightly longer, thicker haft.
- Suggested move: **Thunder blow** - a hammer slam that knocks back everything in front of him.

## Berserker: `barbarian-berserker.svg`
- "Spike bad. Different style": the diamond studs and thin spikes are gone. Now seven clean cone spikes round the
  ball, each lit on one side, and a ring of five short spikes on the top face leaning out from a bright crown, the
  way a spiked ball looks from above.
- Move (the designer's, from the roster): **Ground slam** - slams the ground, sending a repeating shockwave forward.
- Weak: at 40px the ball reads as a grey star. Clearer than round 3, but the top spikes are a detail.
