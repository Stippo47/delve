# Banshee, round 3 (new enemy, from the Hollow's head)

The note was "keep the head for a ghoul/banshee enemy, doesn't work as a skeleton" and the brief says "wispy hair or a
hood". That is a real either/or, so there are two labelled versions. They share everything except the hair or hood.

Both versions:
- The head is the Hollow's skull, unchanged: tall and narrow, a pointed chin, the biggest and deepest sockets of the set. It sits 6 lower than round 2 to make room for the trail.
- Body: one pale ghost shape (white to cool teal, lit from the left). It is widest at the shoulders and trails back (up) into three wavy wisps that turn lighter toward the tips. No legs.
- Arms reach forward: a ragged sleeve on each side, ending in a bony hand with three long splayed claws.
- Parts: `#body` (trail, hair or hood, skull), `#arm-l` and `#arm-r` (sleeve and claw hand, pivots 52 114 and 148 114), both drawn in front. No weapon, so nothing goes behind the body.
- The outline filter is the round-3 one, exactly.

## `banshee-hair.svg`
- Long lavender-white hair, parted over the dome. Two long locks hang beside the face down to the shoulders, and a back mass streams behind into two tips over the ghost trail.
- Still weak: at 40px the lavender hair and the teal body are close in value, so it reads as one pale blob with a skull. The dome is mostly covered by hair, so less of the Hollow's skull shape shows.

## `banshee-hood.svg`
- A ragged hood made from the same ghost cloth. It covers the dome and comes to a soft peak at the back that runs into the middle wisp. The face shows in its opening, with a shadow band under the hood's edge. Ragged drapes hang beside the cheeks.
- At 40px this one reads the clearest: a sheet ghost with a skull face.
- Still weak: the hood hides the top of the skull, so the Hollow's tall dome only shows as the face.

## Open questions
- The skull is still warm bone. A cooler, ghost-tinted skull would tie in with the body, but it would also stand out less.
- The sockets are empty. Small glowing pinpoints (monsters may have eyes) would help it read as a ghost; I left them out to keep the head as it was.
