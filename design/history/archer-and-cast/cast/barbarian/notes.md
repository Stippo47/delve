# Barbarian: 4 designs

1. **Wildmane**: no headgear. A huge swept-back rust mane that runs into a shaggy beard around a small calm face. A greatsword is carried over the left shoulder: the blade sits behind the mane and both fists are on the gold hilt at front-left.
2. **Bearskin**: a bear's head worn as a helm, with round ears sticking out at the back corners, the snout over the brow and two fangs framing the face. One forepaw of the hide hangs over the left shoulder with its claws forward. A spiked wooden club is in the right fist.
3. **Bullskull**: a bull skull worn as a full mask, so no face shows. Its dark horns sweep out and then forward like a charge. A double-bit greataxe is yoked across the shoulders behind the head, with both wrists hooked over the haft.
4. **Topknot**: shaved head with ears, a long topknot tail with a gold ring streaming behind, and a forked beard. He holds a hand axe in each fist at the front corners, with the blades turned outward.

## Still weak
- Wildmane: at 40px the two-layer mane breaks into a lot of points. It reads as "spiky orange ball + sword" more than as hair. The swept-back locks help, but it still sits closer to the archer's leaf than I'd like.
- Bearskin: it looks cute and teddy-like, not fierce. The front is crowded (muzzle, fangs, face, paw and club all sit at the front). The draped paw reads a bit like a sausage arm. The fangs and the bear's mouth sit right above the face, so check that it doesn't read as an open mouth. I lightened the face shadow to reduce that.
- Bullskull: strongest silhouette at 40px, but the skull is a plain cream shape with no surface detail. Only the hands show there's a person under it. It's also wide: the axe head reaches the left edge of the viewBox.
- Topknot: the bald head is a large flat peach dome. It's clean but plain, and a bit egg- or baby-like at big size. I tried war paint (meridian stripes, a single slash, a centre stripe, two parallel stripes) and dropped it: each version read as a pumpkin, scratches, an eye, or a pair of eyes.
- Bearskin's club sticks out to the right, so its content is off-centre (x 25..206). It still rotates fine around (100,100).
