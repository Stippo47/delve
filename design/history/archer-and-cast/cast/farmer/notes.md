# Farmer — 4 designs

1. **Haymaker** (design-1.svg): a wide straw hat with a wavy brim, red band and a blue sewn-on patch. A pitchfork is carried like a pike on the right side. The pole runs under the brim and sticks out at the back-right. The tines point forward.
2. **Digger** (design-2.svg): a puffy blue flat cap with a dark peak and spiky ginger hair poking out at the sides. A shovel is held level across the waist like a bat, with the spade jutting out to the right.
3. **Reaper's Daughter** (design-3.svg): a red headscarf with a big two-eared knot at the front, and dark hair framing the face. A scythe goes up the left side, and the long blade hooks over the back of the head.
4. **Market Day** (design-4.svg): a plum knit beanie with a pompom and a turned-up cuff, plus a wicker back-basket with a pumpkin, a cabbage and carrot tops in it. A rake is held two-handed and pointed forward-left.

## Still weak
- All four look smaller and plainer than the archer. Each body is about 120 units across as the brief asks, but the archer's petals and bow make it look bigger. Scaling them up would push the tools out of the viewBox.
- The face on every design is only a chin showing under the brim, cap peak or scarf. It is calm but small, and at 40px it is just a skin-coloured dot.
- Design 2: the cap is one big flat blue shape, and at 40px the hair spikes make it look a bit like a sea urchin.
- Design 3: at 40px the head is a plain red disc. The knot only reads at 70px and up, so the scythe has to carry the character.
- Design 3: the scythe blade is at the back (shown as a wind-up), so the weapon does not point where the sprite aims.
- Design 4: most of the rake shaft is hidden, so the rake head looks a bit detached. The pompom still shows as a light dot in the middle.
- Things I tried and dropped: a tin pail helmet (looked like a red button), a bald grandpa with a beard (drab, didn't say "farmer"), braids on design 3 (looked like arms), and polka dots on design 3 (looked like a toadstool).
