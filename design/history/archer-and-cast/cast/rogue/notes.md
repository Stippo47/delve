# Rogue: 4 designs

1. **Ribbon Tails**: round head bound in a crimson bandana, with a knot at the back-left whose two streamers blow back (speed). Twin daggers held forward and splayed like fangs.
2. **Cutpurse**: a slate-blue flat cap whose stiff peak juts out over the face, so only the chin shows. A fat loot sack is hauled over the left shoulder by its neck, and one dagger is held out front-right.
3. **Highwayman**: a gold-trimmed plum tricorn worn point-forward, so the front corner is the facing arrow. The face sits in the hat's shadow. Dagger in reverse grip with the blade sweeping back along the right flank, and a smoke bomb in the left hand for the teleport.
4. **Knife Fan**: bare head with silver hair swept back into flame spikes and a short teal shoulder cape. A fan of three throwing knives in the right hand, with the left hand leading.

## Still weak
- All four are smaller than the archer, whose petals and bow make it ~185 wide. At 40px the rogues look a size down. That suits "nimble", but if they need to match, scale them up ~8%.
- D1: the knot reads a bit like a beanie pom-pom. At 40px the two streamers can look like horns from some angles.
- D2: the cap is one big flat blue mass, and the mouth almost disappears under the peak. At 40px the sack plus hand reads as a lollipop shape more than a sack. It's the weakest of the four.
- D3: the strongest at 40px (a clear triangle with a gold edge). The fuse on the bomb is the one small detail. The point-forward corner is a point, but it faces forward and the shape is a triangle, not a leaf.
- D4: pale overall, and the spiky hair is a stock anime look. At big size the knife fan can read as a claw. The light fringe ridge across the brow can look like a headband.
