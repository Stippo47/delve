# Knight — 4 designs (steel + royal blue + archer gold)

1. **Vanguard**: closed, rounded great helm with a gold crusader cross brace and no face. A big chevron heater shield is braced across the front, and an arming sword is thrust forward past the shield's right edge. A shield-first charger.
2. **Errant**: open-faced armet with a curling blue feather panache, a heater shield slung diagonally on the back, and a two-handed greatsword held low and diagonal across the front. A swordsman with a plume.
3. **Norman**: round open helm with a gold brow band on a broad mail mantle that gives him wide, heavy shoulders. A tall kite shield runs point-forward down the left side and the sword is held out right in a low sweeping guard.
4. **Boar**: blue-enamelled bascinet with a long pointed steel "pig-face" visor jutting forward (closed, no face). The sword is cocked back over the left shoulder ready to swing, and a round buckler is punched out in the right fist.

## Still weak
- All four use the same steel/blue/gold palette, so they differ by design and not by colour. A team colour still needs choosing.
- D1: the helm is a plain dome with a cross. Up close it can read as a ball, and at 40px it is mostly "a shield with a head". The hand perched on the shield's top-left edge is a bit stiff.
- D2: the busiest silhouette (plume, back shield and greatsword). The sword hands overlap the right cheek, and the plume's spiky tufts are close to "small detail".
- D3: the mail mantle only shows on the right because the kite hides the left, so it can read as a grey lump. The face is small. A nasal bar was tried and cut because it read as a tooth or nose.
- D4: the egg-plus-snout outline is the archer's teardrop pointing the other way (to the front). It is very readable for facing but has no face, which makes it colder than the others.
- Overall the knights are a little smaller and less organic than the archer. Bumping the body scale about 5% would match its presence better.
- A kettle-hat design was tried and rejected because from above it becomes concentric circles that read as a target.
