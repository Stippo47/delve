- variant-1 (Bold cut): one heavy outer silhouette line with thin inner lines, and split-tone "folded leaf" shading (lit left half, shaded right half) in place of the offset crescent and sheen. The heavy outline keeps the shape crisp at 40px at every rotation, and the fold leans into the leaf-hood idea.
- variant-2 (Soft tonal): smooth gradients in place of hard shading, and every outline inked in a deep shade of its own colour (dark green, amber, brown) instead of near-black. The brim's shadow fades softly down the eyeless face. It's a softer, more polished mobile-icon finish on the same shapes.
- variant-3 (Bubbly): a rounded gumdrop hood tip, a plump rolled brim with a smaller face window and only a thin shade sliver, mitten (rounded-rect) hands, and a chunkier bow with ball nocks. It's a cuter, chunkier shape language that still reads as the same archer.
