- variant-1: Lineless interior with one bold outer outline, soft radial/linear gradients replacing the hard shadow crescent and the sheen streak. Parts are separated by colour, not by inner lines, so the silhouette reads first at 40px and the look gets softer and more modern.
- variant-2: Bubblier hood with a softened tip, the two cape lobes replaced by a fan of layered leaf petals (two tones, with midribs), and rounded-rectangle mitten hands. It leans into the leaf/nature identity and gives a cuter, chunkier, more toy-like silhouette that stays recognisable at every rotation.
- variant-3: Faceless ranger. The eyes are removed and the brim's sagging shadow hides the upper face so only the lit chin and nose show. Shading is flat two-tone, split down the hood seam (light left, dark right, no highlight), applied the same way to the brim, bow and hands. The bow is a chunkier tapered flatbow with dark nocks and a leather grip. Moodier and more graphic, but still clean flat vector.
