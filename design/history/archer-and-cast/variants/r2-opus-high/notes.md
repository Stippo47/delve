- attempt-1 (merge): Heavy outer outline plus thin inner lines. The hood is flat two-tone split down the leaf vein, with side veins angled toward the tip. Two rows of swept leaf petals on the shoulders, a chunky tapered flatbow with dark tips and a leather grip, rounded-rect hands. The face has no eyes or blush: the brim casts a mid-tone skin shadow that follows the arch, with a dark-green hood lining at the rim, so it reads as a calm face, not an open mouth.
- attempt-2 (lineless soft): Branches off Opus V1. One heavy outer outline and no inner lines at all. Parts are separated by colour and soft offset contact shadows. The split is kept, but each half is a gentle gradient. The face shadow fades smoothly from warm brown into skin, so there is no hard band.
- attempt-3 (pointed leaf cowl): The gold brim and face opening become leaf-shaped, pointing up to the clasp, and the brim shadow follows that point. One clean swept petal per shoulder instead of two rows. Uniform 4px lines everywhere and no heavy silhouette, for a lighter, more graphic shape language.
- attempt-4 (stacked leaves): The hood is two layered leaves, a dark outer leaf and a lighter, smaller veined leaf on top with a small cast shadow. The shading comes from that layering instead of a left/right split, and the brim, bow and hands get bottom-edge shade to match. Heavy outer outline plus thin inner lines, as in the merge.
