# Base human, set A: 4 bases (face, hair and hands only)

1. **Bowl** (base-1.svg): a big round skull (r70) under a smooth brown bowl cut with a chunky scalloped fringe and a small round bun at the back of the crown. The face is the lower crescent under a deep fringe. Small round ears are tucked under the sideburns. Two fists are held up symmetrically at the front corners, just under the chin.
2. **Swept** (base-2.svg): a narrow egg-shaped head with a bare forehead and the largest, most open face. Blonde hair has one heavy side-swept fringe lock with pointed tips falling across the brow to the left cheek. The rest is combed back into a thick rounded tail that trails back-right. Small neat ears. Both hands are angled together at front-left, ready for a two-handed grip (spear or staff).
3. **Crop** (base-3.svg): a broad squarish head with a wide jaw. Short tufty copper crop with a blunt lobed fringe, cut high above big jug ears. Heavy square fists (32x30) are held out wide at the front corners. Brawler proportions.
4. **Mop** (base-4.svg): a small face sunk deep in a huge two-layer cloud of dark-brown lobed curls. The hair hides the ears and falls past the cheeks. The hands are asymmetric: one leads close in front of the chin, and the other rests out at the right side on the hair.

## Still weak
- Bowl: at 90°/270° and at 40px it reads a bit like a coffee bean or chestnut (a dark dome with a pale bite out of it). The bun is small and only just shows at 40px. The two fists under the chin can read as paws or feet.
- Swept: it's the narrowest of the four, so at 40px it looks a size smaller than the archer and the others. The two angled hands at front-left can look like a paw, and they cover part of the jaw. The tail does most of the work for facing readability; the face is a big plain skin area.
- Crop: the face is a very large flat skin plane with only a tiny mouth. It risks looking blank or baby-faced at big size. The wide fists plus jug ears make it read "crab-like" at 45° rotations.
- Mop: it's the darkest overall. The top of the face is shadowed and pointed by the hair opening, so at 40px the face can read like a beak or snout pointing forward. The side hand sits on the hair and could read as a detached block.
- All four: the fringe contact shadow is lighter than the archer's hood shadow on purpose (bare heads). Check that it still matches once the class headgear goes back on.
