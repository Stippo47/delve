# Base human, set B: 4 designs (head + face + hair + hands only)

1. **Bowl**: a round head under a heavy slate-black bowl cut. The fringe is split into 3 lobes, the ears peek out below the rim, the face is a wide open band, and two small fists sit at the front corners.
2. **Long**: a long egg-shaped head that is longer than it is wide. Blond hair is slicked back to a soft widow's peak. A ponytail falls off the back and swings over the right shoulder. Big ears stick out sideways, and open hands are held wide at the flanks.
3. **Cloud**: a huge curly hair cloud made of puffs that swallows the head. A small low face is tucked under the front puffs, with no ears. The hands are pressed together just under the chin.
4. **Jaw**: a wide-jawed head with a narrow crown, broad cheeks and a square front. Short ginger hair is tufty at the back, with one big fringe swooping forward-left. The face is large and open, the ears are small, and both mitts grip off to the front-right like a two-handed grip.

## Still weak
- Bowl: the safest of the four and the cleanest at 40px, but plain. It's a mushroom or helmet shape, and the bowl could be mistaken for a cap. The faint crown-part lens at the seam is the only interior tone.
- Long: the hairline shadow under the widow's peak makes a V that reads a little like a frowning brow at big size. The ponytail can read as a plume or flame. At 40px it's distinctive: the tail curl plus the wide hands give a clear direction.
- Cloud: the best silhouette, but at 40px the face is a sliver. Facing is carried mostly by the pair of hands, which is weak from some angles. The dark hair also loses some edge against the outline.
- Jaw: the swoop edge plus the hard split seam makes an S/X pattern. At 40px it reads a bit like a beach ball or yin-yang, and the large face area is blank. The back tufts are a stock spiky-anime shape. The off-centre grip suits a 2-hander but breaks symmetry for one-hand classes.
- All four: sized up 4-10% around (100,100) to match the archer's presence. They are still a touch narrower than the archer, whose petals and bow reach x 8..192.
