## Still weak
- The faces are big plain skin areas, carried only by the gradient and the mouth. Mop and Egg especially can look baby- or egg-like up close. Curtains is the closest to the archer's "face in a frame" proportion.
- At 40px, Mop and Cloud are mostly "a coloured ball plus hand bumps". The hands carry the facing direction. The ears on Mop barely show.
- On Egg + Bun at small sizes, the bun, the two hands and the ears read as several similar bumps (a bit mouse-ear-like). The bun is the only feature that marks the back.
- Curtains: the stacked hands still touch the chin, so at a glance they can look like they come from under the mouth.
- Hand sizes are bigger than the archer's (about 32x29 against 24x21) so they read at 40px. They may need trimming once weapons go in.
- Things I tried and dropped: a full beard (it made a lens-shaped skin slit that read as an EYE when rotated). The same beard joined to the hair with sideburns (it became a hood or balaclava, which re-skins the archer's layout). An ear-to-ear widow's-peak hairline with a ponytail (it read as an onion or flame).
- All four are scaled 1.06–1.1 to match the archer's presence. Each spans about 186–206 units and stays inside the viewBox.
