# Character direction (all four variants)
Each unit IS a head: the circle body is a big chibi cartoon head (hood/helmet/hair/ears built onto it),
with a big expressive cartoon face — large eyes with highlights, small mouth, bold readable expressions
(determined idle, gritted teeth on attack, X-eyes or swirl on death). Mobile tower-defense chibi energy:
cute, punchy, saturated, readable at small size. Props (bow, shield+sword, daggers) float/are held beside
the head — no bodies or limbs, at most tiny floating hands. Original designs only.
The ONLY thing that differs between the four variants is how facing direction is shown (see your brief).
