# Art round 8 (lane A, after the round-9 answers): the art-round7 notes, then real art for the placeholder kinds

Part 1 starts from the exact file the designer saw: the round-7 generators were copied into `_src/` (`E3/`, `E1r4/`)
and checked to rebuild the round-7 files (and the round-6 troll, the game's troll until now) byte for byte before
anything changed; each change sits behind a switch (`ORC_R8`, `GHOST_R8`, `TROLL_R8`, `CROC_R8`) with the note quoted,
and `_src/check8.py` turns them off and checks the copies still give the files the designer saw, then checks
`_src/build8.py` rebuilds this folder byte for byte. Part 2 is new art in the heroes' bust style, built with the A2
library the heroes were made with (`_src/A2/wild8.py`, `bandits8.py`, `bandits8_more.py`, `grove8.py`, `sewer8.py`).
Renders: `<folder>/renders/<name>.png` (the board: big beside the archer, 8 facings, game size) with `<name>-before.png`
beside it: the tile the note was on (part 1), or the placeholder the game showed, in its variant's colours (part 2,
`_src/befores.py`). Scripts: `_src/build8.py`, `_src/render8.py`, `_src/check8.py`, `_src/game8.py` (the game copies),
`_src/look.py` (scratch look-over sheets into `tools/riffle/out/r8`).

## Part 1: the notes
- **Orc** (`enemies/greenskin-orc.svg`), "Wider mouth": the mouth line runs x 74..126 (it ran 84..116), same height, bow and line; the tusks stay where they were, still coming up out of the mouth. Nothing else.
- **Ghost A** and **Ghost B** (`enemies/ghost-a.svg`, `ghost-b.svg`), "Remove the sliver on their back for this and the other ghost I liked": the narrow darker fold that ran from the middle of the head back into the tail is gone on both. Nothing else.
- **Troll** (`enemies/greenskin-troll.svg`, its boulder `props/troll-boulder.svg`, the board `boards/greenskin-troll-boulder.svg`), on the round-6 troll ("Liked this ones overall shape more. Fix face and make like 2x bigger overall and will look pretty good. Also needs a boulder"): the round-6 hump, moss patch, small head and ears as they were; the face: the drooping nose (it read as a trunk) and the dark patch above it (a hole) are gone, a big round nose and a heavy underbite with two small tusks coming up out of the mouth instead; the boulder: both fists reach forward on long arms and rest on a big round grey boulder with a tuft of moss (the game's boulder is its own object, so it is a prop of its own; the log club is gone, the boulder is its weapon); 2x: in the game it is now twice the archer across (it was 1.4x; js/enemies.js r 42 -> 60, vr 31 -> 44, mass 3 -> 6), and its board shows it at that size beside the archer.
- **Troll A, mohawk -> the Chieftain** (`enemies/greenskin-chieftain.svg`), "Keep this but not as a troll": kept exactly (the same shapes and colours, only its comment differs) and made the Warren's chieftain, the boss the designer renamed ("The Warboss: Chieftain. More abilities."): the red war crest, the big tusks and the club read as the tribe's chief, and beside the Warren's hunched trolls it stands upright on its shoulders. In `art/enemies/chieftain.svg` for the boss lane; the game still draws the Warboss as the orc.

## Part 2: the kinds that wore placeholders
- **Wolf** (`enemies/wild-wolf.svg`): the designer on Call Wolf, "capsule shaped ... too complex ... match the werewolf": one rounded capsule of a warm grey body, a bushy tail with a dark tip, the werewolf's round skull, laid-back ears with the dusky inner ear, long snout with a pale muzzle and dark nose, his scalloped ruff and his paws with three short white claws. The druid's called wolf wears it too.
- **Dire wolf** (`enemies/wild-dire-wolf.svg`): the wolf in black and smoke grey with a mane of pointed tufts lying back over the shoulders (the game draws it 1.25x).
- **The Alpha** (`enemies/wild-alpha.svg`): the giant wolf in pale silver, a great tufted mane, one ear torn, an old scar across the brow in one darker shade, bigger paws.
- **Bandit, sword and shield** (`enemies/bandit-sword.svg`): the gang's look on the heroes' base (rag-edged leather tunic, the Bandit hero's red bandanna, no eyes), brown hair under the navy head scarf knotted at the back; a short sword under the right shoulder, a round wooden buckler with an iron boss in the left hand.
- **Torch bandit** (`enemies/bandit-torch.svg`): the same gang look under a rust-brown hood with its tail lying back; a short sword and a burning torch held out in front.
- **Greatsword bandit** (`enemies/bandit-greatsword.svg`): an iron kettle hat with a round brim; a big two-handed sword held in front the Old Guard's way, both fists on the long grip, the wide blade across the front.
- **Axe bandit** (`enemies/bandit-axe.svg`): a fur cap with a pale fur rim; a big hand axe under the right shoulder, the blade turned in (the designer's way with the werewolf's hatchet), the left hand free.
- **Spear bandit** (`enemies/bandit-spear.svg`): a leather cap with a red feather lying back; a long spear under the right shoulder, the point well forward.
- **Knife bandit** (`enemies/bandit-daggers.svg`): a dark slate hood drawn low; a dagger in each hand, both under the shoulders, a little apart.
- **Poacher** (`enemies/bandit-poacher.svg`): a woodland hunter, not one of the masked gang: a drab olive hood (not the archer's leaf green) with its tail lying back, a short scruffy beard, a grey fur mantle; a short dark recurve bow in front with its string (the game draws it to the drawing hand) and an arrow; a rolled net on the back for the net it throws.
- **Bandit brute** (`enemies/bandit-brute.svg`): the gang's heavy: bald and scarred over the red bandanna, broad patched leather shoulders with a shaggy collar; a big studded club with short iron spikes, a bare fist in leather wraps.
- **The Bandit Chief** (`enemies/bandit-chief.svg`): a black tricorn with a gold edge and a red plume, the gang's red bandanna, a black coat with a high collar and gold trim, throwing knives on a bandolier over the left shoulder; a gold-hilted cutlass and a smoke bomb with a lit fuse.
- **Thornback boar** (`enemies/wild-boar.svg`): the wolves' capsule build: a dark bristly body with two staggered rows of big pale thorns down the spine (the thorns its elite sheds), a tufted tail, small ears, a pink-grey snout disc, two big curved tusks (a #front part, over the swinging front legs), two hooves.
- **Sproutling** (`enemies/wild-sproutling.svg`): a small walking sapling: a round green bud with two big seed leaves fanned back over it and a curled shoot between them, a collar of small leaves for shoulders, twig arms ending in leafy hands. No face.
- **Ent** (`enemies/wild-ent.svg`): a walking tree: a leafy crown on its back (#back, sways), bark shoulders with moss, a bark head with a dark knot-hole mouth and two branch stubs from the brow, thick branch arms with knotty wooden fists and twig fingers.
- **Wisp** (`enemies/wild-wisp.svg`): a floating light: a round glowing lime orb, a flickering flame tail streaming back (#back), two little wisps of flame for hands. No face.
- **The Elder Ent** (`enemies/wild-elder-ent.svg`), big, built in parts: a great crown of dark leaves over its back (#back, sways), a massive grey-brown trunk with moss and pale shelf fungus on the shoulders, antler-like branches from the brow, a broad bark face with a deep knot-hole mouth over a ragged mossy beard, two huge branch arms with root claws (arm-l, arm-r, swing).
- **Rat king** (`enemies/sewer-rat-king.svg`), "a jumble of rats ... more effort than the rat sprite layered on itself": a heap of rats of different sizes, furs and poses (each with its own round rump, pointed head turned on its neck, round pink ears, pink nose), heads out unevenly on all sides, their pink tails knotted over the middle and loose ends hanging out behind; the king on top at the front, biggest, with a little gold crown; the two front rats are the arm parts (they lunge when it bites), the two at the back sway. The game no longer adds the springy rat tail to it.
- **Croc** (`enemies/sewer-croc.svg`), "Has 2 axes, bite is an ability": the picked Croc A (round 7) with a heavy axe in each clawed fist, both under the shoulders, the blades turned in; the bone club and the turtle shell are gone (`CROC_R8`).
- **Sewer crab** (`enemies/sewer-crab.svg`): a wide rusty-red shell with a smooth armoured front lip ("armoured from the front"), four jointed legs a side (#back), two big pincers forward (arm parts, they snap when it pinches); its blue and green kinds are this art turned by the game (hue 180 and 90), which lands on blue and green.

## In the game (`_src/game8.py`, and the defs)
- `art/enemies/`: orc.svg (the Orc and the Warboss), ghost.svg (Ghost A is now the Crypt's ghost; Ghost B is `ghost-b.svg`, unused, for the cold ghost the roster asks for), troll.svg (the troll and the rock troll), chieftain.svg (unused), and new files for every part-2 kind; `art/props/troll-boulder.svg`; `art/summons/wolf.svg` (the called wolf).
- js/wildwood.js, js/foes9.js, js/elderent.js point at them, the recolours that faked them are gone, and headR is set so the body fills the collider (body half-width x vr / r): wolf 35 (also the called wolf), dire wolf 35 (x1.25), Alpha 40, bandits and poacher 54, brute 64, chief 54, boar 41, sproutling 34, ent 59, wisp 34, Elder Ent 66, rat king 66, croc 56, crab 57, wolf rider 34 (the new wolf recoloured).
- The troll: r 60, vr 44, mass 6; its boulder is the `troll-boulder` prop, held in its fists: foes9.js `HOLD` puts a held boulder 0.46 of the troll's radius inside its collider, and sim.js doesn't push a troll and its own held (or still rolling) boulder apart or steer it round it. render.js draws the springy rat tail only on hand-drawn rats.
- Checked on `python -m http.server 8871`: `tools/labshot.mjs` sheets of every kind in every pose, lab fights (`&foes=` added to the lab's fight mode) with the trolls and boulders and the Sewer kinds, and `tools/clip.mjs` runs in the Wildwood (`theme=wildwood&allnodes=1`) and its boss room: no page errors.

## Still weak
- The troll's "2x" is read as twice the archer (on the board they looked the same size); in the game it is 1.45x the old troll. The arms reaching forward to the boulder change its outline at the front; the club is gone.
- The Chieftain is a name and a place, not a change: if "not as a troll" meant it should look like something else, its teal skin is still the trolls' colour.
- The wolves' ruff and the dire wolf's and Alpha's tufted manes are the busiest parts at game size; the Alpha's white mane edge reads a little like a collar.
- The bandits differ by headgear and weapon only (the gang shares the red bandanna); the poacher's bow sits where the archer's does, smaller and darker.
- The greatsword is short for a greatsword (the box limits it); the spear and daggers are thin at game size.
- The rat king's many pink ears and noses make it busy; it reads as a heap of rats up close, a spiky brown blob at game size.
- The Elder Ent and the ent share their build; the Elder Ent is set apart by its grey bark, antlers, beard, fungus and size.
- Part-2 boards are drawn at the box size like every round; only the troll's board is at its game size.
