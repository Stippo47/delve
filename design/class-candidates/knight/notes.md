# Knight — 4 designs on the approved base (set-a8, Bowl head, 160 shoulders)

1. **Paladin**: a young knight in an open steel sallet with a gold comb. A blue cape with a scalloped hem flares out behind him, and he has round steel pauldrons. He carries a gold-rimmed blue heater shield on his left arm and holds an arming sword forward in his right hand.
2. **Old Guard**: a bald, grey-templed veteran with a big white spade beard and moustache, which stand in for the mouth. He wears a cream fur collar on a crimson mantle and heavy pauldrons. A crimson heater shield is slung on his back, and he grips a greatsword two-handed, angled forward-right.
3. **Bastion**: a faceless hulk in a flat-topped great helm with a visor slit and a gold cross. He has stacked dark-iron pauldrons and a red surcoat. A big red-and-gold pavise braced in front works as a shield-ram for the charge, and a short sword pokes out on the right.
4. **Dame**: a bareheaded woman knight. Her auburn hair is parted and swept back into a long braid that trails behind her, and a teal cape sweeps off to the right. She wears silver pauldrons, carries a kite shield on her right arm, and holds a sword forward in her left hand, so she is mirrored from the Paladin.

## Still weak
- D1: the cape is still a broad blue block behind him, and the scalloped hem reads a little like a cloud. The helm and pauldrons are close in value. It is the most "default knight" of the four.
- D2: the back shield peeks out as a red wedge and can read as an ear or horn, especially at 40px. The pommel sits on the cheek, and the crimson mantle barely shows.
- D3: very blocky and boxy, which is intentional for a tank but less organic than the archer. Up close the studs and slit are the smallest details. At 40px it mostly reads as a red and grey crate with a cross.
- D4: the hair cap is helmet-like in shape. The braid and cape carry the read.
- All four share the same face treatment and the same steel ramp, and the weapons are fairly small compared with the archer's bow.
